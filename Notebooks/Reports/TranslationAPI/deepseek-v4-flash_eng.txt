In this village, I held a meeting with a total of 9 farmers. In the meeting, I first gave information about myself. I gave them complete information about my app and my organization. After providing the information, I talked with the farmer brothers. In the conversation, I first asked them about their main crops. The farmer brothers said they grow wheat, cotton, maize, and in the rainy season, sugarcane. They do not grow potatoes. After that, I asked the farmer brothers about soil testing. None of the farmers had any information about soil testing. After that, I asked the farmer brothers about KCC (Kisan Credit Card). None of the farmers had any information about the KCC center. After that, I asked about the app – whether they use this app. None of the farmer brothers had any information about the app either. Then I asked them about crop nutrients. Regarding crop nutrients, after that I asked the farmer brothers about camps held in the village. The farmer brothers said that camps are held by the government, but they only come for promotion of their own seeds and fertilizers. After that, I asked the farmer brothers about seeds. Many farmers bring seeds from the local market, and some farmers get them from the society. They get seeds, fertilizers, etc., from the society. After that, I asked the farmer brothers about weeds, etc. After that, in this village, the same problem exists as in every village: when a notice for spraying comes, they have to buy other products along with the spray, which the farmer brothers are not even aware of. After that, I asked the farmer brothers about wild animals. There is no damage from wild animals in this village; only stray livestock cause damage to crops. After that, I asked about diseases in wheat. They mentioned only a yellow rust disease. After that, they mentioned about kangura (?) and then I asked about sugarcane. In sugarcane, the farmer brothers mentioned many diseases before the arrival of the sab (?) – they mentioned red rot, and also mentioned whitefly disease. After that, I asked about maize. In maize, the farmer brothers mentioned stem borer and the borer that goes inside the cob, and also the problem of stem borer in the fodder that is sown for livestock. I got information about these crops. Apart from these, no information about any other crop was obtained in this village.

After that, in the demo, whatever questions the farmers asked about our app, and whatever answers our app gave to the farmer brothers, they liked them very much. Proper answers were given. After conducting the app demo, I took feedback from the farmer brothers. In the feedback, I asked them to install the app on their phones. Then I asked the farmer brothers about their suggestions – what questions they have. The farmer brothers said that firstly, they should be given information about good seeds, and the problems of those who do proper farming should be solved. After that, the farmer brothers said that they should be given some benefit regarding crop nutrients as well. After these discussions, I took a group photo of the farmer brothers. After taking the group photo, I talked with some farmers.
