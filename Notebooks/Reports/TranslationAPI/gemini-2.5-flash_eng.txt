In this village, I held a meeting with a total of nine farmers. In the meeting, I first introduced myself. I gave them complete information about our app, Azar Sakha. After providing the information, I conversed with the farmer brothers. In the conversation, I first asked the farmer brothers about their main crops. The farmer brothers mentioned wheat and maize, and that sugarcane and potato cultivation is not done in the village.

After that, I asked the farmer brothers about soil testing. No farmer had any information about soil testing. After that, I asked the farmer brothers about KCC (Kisan Credit Card). No farmer had any information about KCC. After that, I asked about the app, if they use such an app. No farmer brother had any information about any such app. Then I asked them about crop insurance.

After that, I asked the farmer brothers about the camps held in the village. Regarding the camps, the farmer brothers said that camps are held, but they are usually for promoting their own seeds or medicines. After that, I asked the farmer brothers about seeds. They said that many farmers buy seeds from the local market, and some farmers buy from societies. They get seeds and fertilizers etc. from these societies.

After that, I asked the farmer brothers about other issues. In this village too, the same problem exists as in every village: when there is a shortage of urea, they are forced to buy other products along with urea, which the farmer brothers do not need. After that, I asked the farmer brothers about wild animals. There is no damage from wild animals in this village. In this village, crops are only damaged by stray animals.

After that, I asked about diseases affecting wheat. They only mentioned one disease: yellow rust. After that, they mentioned 'Kaunge Aari'. After that, I asked about stem diseases. Regarding stem diseases, the farmer brothers first mentioned dwarf disease, then aphids/jassids, and also Chanda disease. After that, I asked about maize. Regarding maize, the farmer brothers mentioned the 'gob' (whorl) worm and the worm that gets inside the cob, and also the problem of 'veer' worm in the fodder sown for animals. Information was received about these crops. No information about any other crop was received in this village.

During the app demo, the questions asked by the farmers and the answers given by our app to the farmer brothers were very well received and properly answered. After conducting the app demo, I took feedback from the farmer brothers. They requested to install the app on their phones. Then I asked the farmer brothers what questions they had for us. In which the farmer brothers said that they should be given information about good quality seeds, and their problems that are properly affecting them should be resolved. After that, the farmer brothers said that they should also be given some benefit from crop insurance.

After these discussions, I took a group photo with the farmer brothers. After taking the group photo, I spoke with one to three farmers individually.
